Daily Skincare Routine

Morning & Night

☀️

Morning

Protect & hydrate
  • 1
    Wet Face Lukewarm water
  • 2
    CeraVe Cream-to-Foam Cleanser
    • Small amount into wet hands, massage gently for 30–60 seconds
    • Wet face cloth → gently wipe cleanser off, rinse cloth, repeat if needed
    • Pat face with skincare towel
    • Mon/Wed/Fri: dry face completely
    • Other days: slightly damp is fine
  • 3
    haruharu Centella 3% PHA Mon / Wed / Fri only — face must be completely dry
    • Pour a dime-sized amount onto a cotton pad, enough to saturate it
    • Gently swipe across forehead, cheeks, nose, and chin — avoid eyes, eyelids, and lips
    • Do not rinse off — let settle 30–60 seconds
  • 4
    Hyaluronic Acid Serum Good Molecules
    • If you used PHA, don't splash water back on — just lightly dampen fingertips
    • 2–3 drops on fingertips, warm between palms first to help it spread further
    • Press/pat over face rather than rubbing — add more drops as needed, no set max
    • Let settle ~30–60 seconds
  • 5
    Caffeine Solution The Ordinary — under-eyes only
    • Tiny drop on fingertip, gently tap under each eye
    • Stay around the under-eye/orbital bone area
    • Do NOT put on eyelids, lash line, or right against your eyeball
  • 6
    Moisturizer CeraVe Moisturizing Cream
    • Small amount, dot around face, gently spread
    • Spreading, not performing the Great Schmear™
  • 7
    SPF 50 Neutrogena Hydro Boost — always last, every day
    • Apply generously and evenly over face and neck
    • Don't forget hairline and ears
    • Let it set before heading outside
🌙

Night

Cleanse & repair
  • 1
    Wet Face Lukewarm water
  • 2
    CeraVe Cream-to-Foam Cleanser
    • Small amount into wet hands, massage gently for 30–60 seconds
    • Wet face cloth → gently wipe cleanser away, rinse cloth, repeat if needed
    • Leave skin slightly damp for HA
  • 3
    Hyaluronic Acid Serum Good Molecules
    • 2–3 drops on fingertips, warm between palms first to help it spread further
    • Press/pat over slightly damp face rather than rubbing — add more drops as needed, no set max
    • Let settle ~30–60 seconds
  • 4
    Super Peptide Serum Good Molecules — every night
    • 2–3 drops on fingertips, thin layer over forehead, cheeks, nose, chin
    • Add 1 more drop only if needed for coverage
    • Let settle ~30–60 seconds
  • 5
    Azelaic Acid Suspension 10% Mon / Wed / Fri only — Tue/Thu/Sat/Sun: skip this step
    • Pea-sized amount on fingertip, small dots on forehead, cheeks, chin
    • Spread into a thin, even layer
    • Avoid eyes, eyelids, lips — let settle about a minute
  • 6
    Moisturizer CeraVe Moisturizing Cream
    • Small amount, dot around face, gently spread over face and neck

Good to know

  • The haruharu is a leave-on exfoliating serum, not a cleanser — applied to completely dry skin after cleansing, Mon/Wed/Fri only.
  • Peptide serum runs every night; Azelaic Acid layers on top Mon/Wed/Fri — the two work fine together, no conflict.
  • Thin layer. Gentle hands. More product ≠ more effective. We're applying skincare, not schmearing condiments onto a sandwich. 😭

😵 I am doing skincare once today

  • Default to the PM routine.
  • Cleanse → HA serum → Peptide serum → Moisturizer.
  • If it's Mon/Wed/Fri: you can do your scheduled Azelaic Acid if your skin is comfortable — or skip it if you're simplifying because you're exhausted.
  • Skip the PHA entirely in the simplified routine.
  • Important: if you're going outside during daylight, sunscreen is still its own thing — put SPF on before you head out even if the rest of your routine happens at night.
