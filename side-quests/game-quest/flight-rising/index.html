Add a dragon

The id is the number at the end of the dragon's profile link. With it, the card shows the dragon and links back to Flight Rising. For the days, use the number in brackets after “cannot breed until” on their profile.

Tap a dragon from the lair to see their tree. Dashed ones live outside it.

Who can breed with whom

Flight Rising won't pair dragons that share an ancestor within five generations, and an ancient breed only pairs with its own breed. This only knows the ancestors on this page, so Flight Rising's breeding screen has the final say.
