The Quest for a Thousand Books

Every book on our shelves, one by one, until there are a thousand of them. Read or not doesn’t matter here — that’s the reading pile’s job. This is the library itself.

Shelving session

One book per line: an ISBN (from the barcode on the back), or a title — “Piranesi by Susanna Clarke” narrows it down. Press Enter to shelve them. A barcode scanner works straight into this box.

    Import from Goodreads

    On Goodreads, go to My Books → Import and export → Export library, download the CSV, and choose it here. You’ll pick which shelves to bring across before anything is added.
