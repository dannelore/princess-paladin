The Rules of Engagement

Read Before You Sit Down

  1. Come with cash

    Ones, fives, tens — twenties if you want a real show. Tips go straight to the queens.

  2. Hand it over, don't tease it

    Wave the bill to get her attention, then let her take it. No pulling back for laughs.

  3. Stay in your seat

    No jumping into a number. Keep your support loud, not physical.

  4. It's her show, not yours

    Enjoy the spotlight from where you're sitting — let each queen have her moment.

  5. Applaud like she can hear it

    Loud, often, and especially at the meet-and-greet after.

  6. Hands off unless invited

    Hair is a hard no. Ask before you hug — the makeup will not survive a white shirt.

  7. Use the pronouns she's giving you

    She'll make it clear — follow her lead, no exceptions.

  8. First-timer nerves are normal

    Nobody's judging you. Just show up ready to have a good time.

  9. Save the photos

    Meet-and-greet is your window. Mid-number is not photo time.

  10. Bring the energy

    Phone down, be present. If you can't have fun, go visit your refrigerator instead.
